# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of personal identity, background, appearance, ability, or level of experience.

## Our standards

Be respectful, empathetic, constructive, and focused on what is best for the community. Harassment, sexualized conduct, trolling, personal attacks, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards. Report incidents privately through a GitHub security advisory or to [@zudaR107](https://github.com/zudaR107).

This policy is adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
